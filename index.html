ABOUT VELT

Built to treat money as a tactile craft.

Velt is a mobile wallet application structured around legible typography, card-driven lighting, and deliberate financial control rather than crowded feeds and promotional clutter.

Purpose

Give everyday users a focused wallet where balances, cards, transfers, offline receipts, and vaults remain one tap away.

Design Philosophy

A cohesive Void & Obsidian dark / Alabaster light canvas using Liquid Glass overlays for context layers, Neumorphic depth for navigation, and tactile hardware controls for actions.

Wallet Experience

Virtual card palettes illuminate the Active Card viewport while Smart Rules, Instant Transfers, and real-time spending cues flow seamlessly.

Main Capabilities

Covers Multi-Card Management, Smart Automation, Spending Discovery, UI Personalization, Receipt Sharing, and Offline Vault storage.
